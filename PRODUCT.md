# Product

**User.** Word lovers, writers, students and teachers who want a trustworthy, deep word origin; and developers,
lexicographers and AI teams who want a structured, cited etymology graph to build on.

**Painful job.** "Where does this word really come from, and how sure is anyone?" Etymonline and Wiktionary give a
paragraph; the OED is paywalled. Nobody exposes each step's evidence, confidence and disputes as data.

**Promise (this edition).** Every reviewed SET-U word history, readable as a plain page that never goes down, and every
dataset downloadable in one click, with a public weekly check that its citations still resolve.

**Wedge.** The live atlas owns the reading experience and search ranking for "[word] etymology". This edition owns the
developer and researcher door: complete files, a SQLite atlas, and a citation-health page that no competitor publishes.

**Why now.** AI companies and educators want structured, cited reference data; the live export API is paginated and its
manifest currently fails.

**Activation metric.** A dataset or SQLite download (GoatCounter event once analytics is on), and inbound links to
`/data/`.

**First revenue event.** None on GitHub Pages. The marked slot is a support link (`SUPPORT_URL`); data licensing for
commercial use, once reuse terms are declared, would run off Pages.

**Won't do.** Compete with set-u.org for search traffic on word pages (they are noindex here), host the intake or review
desks, or declare a licence on Jack's behalf.
